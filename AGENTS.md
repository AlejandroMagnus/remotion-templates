# Agent instructions

When asked to make a video, read `.agents/skills/make-video/SKILL.md` first. Treat VideoSpec as the source of truth, validate before previewing, and preview representative frames before a final render. Never upload or publish a video without explicit user approval.

For planning, writing, or reviewing any public content (including videos, scripts,
and posts), read and apply `docs/valor-humano-y-difusion-responsable.md`. Identify the
viewer benefit internally; do not require a user questionnaire. Respect each
request's specific intent and preserve validated technical components. Mechanical
checks never substitute for editorial judgment, legal review, or rights clearance.

For narration and vocal revisions, also apply `docs/interrogacion-y-naturalidad-vocal.md`.
Keep approved words, accents and question marks intact. Use whole-question delivery
and supported provider controls. Never mark pronunciation or naturalness approved
without listening to the corresponding audio. After regeneration, rerun the existing
timing and scene pipeline; preserve the validated WordBoundary synchronization.
